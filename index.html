Quick Guide
  • Add Audio: Drag .mp3/.wav files anywhere or tap the upload area.
  • Relink: Missing audio shows a dashed circle — click Relink to reconnect a folder.
  • Rank: Drag the ⠿ handle to reorder. Long-press on mobile.
  • Tabs: Drag tabs to reorder (long-press on mobile). Double-click to rename.
  • Search: Filter by rank (#1), title, artist, or notes. Drag disabled while searching.
  • Star: Click ★ to flag tracks in the starred section.
